## 1. Launch ad

Meet Baytak (بيتك, "your home").

Building fees, made clear. Your building's administrator issues a receipt, every resident sees what they owe in USD and LBP, and nobody has to chase a group chat or a paper list.

One receipt for the whole building.
A notification for every resident.
Paid or unpaid, stamped for everyone to see.
Receipts can't be edited once they're issued.

Managing a building? Send us a DM to get set up.
Living in one? Share this with your building's admin.

## 2. Building fees shouldn't take 100 messages

"Did everyone pay?" "Is it dollars or lira?" "Can someone resend the list?"

Sound familiar? With Baytak, your building's administrator issues one receipt and every resident gets it: the amount, the currency, and whether it's paid. No more scrolling back through the group chat.

Send this to whoever runs your building's group chat.

## 3. Bill your whole building in one go

Add the amounts once (maintenance, electricity, elevator, whatever your building charges), send it to everyone or to selected apartments, and add the receipt. Every resident gets a notification.

Managing a building? Send us a DM to get set up.

## 4. Watch a whole building pay up

The most satisfying part of collecting building fees.

In Baytak every apartment is a door plate. Tap one when its fees are paid and it's stamped Paid, floor by floor, until the screen says: Everyone has paid. You always know who has paid and how much is collected.

## 5. Do I owe anything this month?

Open Baytak and the answer is the first thing you see: what you owe, in USD and in LBP, with every receipt behind it. When your administrator marks a receipt paid, it's stamped Paid for you too.

Tag the neighbor who always asks.

## 6. Dollars or lira? Both. Never mixed.

Every amount in Baytak shows its currency, and USD and LBP totals are always kept apart. No conversions, and no guessing which one you owe.

## 7. Receipts can't be changed

Can someone change the amount after it's sent? No.

Once a receipt is issued in Baytak, it can't be edited or deleted. The administrator and every resident see the same record, so there's nothing to argue about later.

## 8. No passwords to forget

Sign in to Baytak with your phone number and a 6-digit code sent by text. That's it. Your phone number is your account.

Running your building's fees? Send us a DM.

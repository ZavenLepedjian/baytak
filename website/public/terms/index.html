What Baytak does

Baytak keeps a record of a residential building's fees: administrators issue receipts and mark them paid, and residents see what they owe. Baytak does not collect, hold or transfer money. Payments happen between residents and their building administrator, outside Baytak.

Your account

  • You sign in with a phone number you own and a code sent to it. Keep your phone secure. Actions taken from your account are your responsibility.
  • Give accurate information, including your apartment when you ask to join a building.
  • Administrator access is granted only after we review the request.

Building administrators

  • You are responsible for the receipts you issue and the payment statuses you record. Receipts cannot be edited or deleted once issued.
  • Use residents' information only to manage your building's fees and to contact residents about them.
  • Send reminders only about real, unpaid receipts.

Using Baytak in ChatGPT

Connecting Baytak to ChatGPT is optional. ChatGPT's answers can be wrong: the Baytak app is the record of what you owe and what was issued. Review every preview before you confirm an action such as issuing a receipt. Your use of ChatGPT is also governed by OpenAI's terms.

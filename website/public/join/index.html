How to join

  1. Install Baytak from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open this page again on the same phone and tap Open in the Baytak app. Sign in with your phone number; a code arrives by SMS.
  3. Enter your apartment and floor, and send your request. Your building administrator approves it, then you see your receipts.

If the button does nothing

Open Baytak yourself, go to Account, then My apartment, and type the building code into Have an invite code?. The code only names the building: nobody joins until the administrator approves.

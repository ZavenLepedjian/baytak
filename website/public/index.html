From one receipt to everyone paid.

  1. Register the building

    The administrator adds the building, its floors and a receipt format: which fields, in which currency.

  2. Approve residents

    Residents find the building and ask to join their apartment. Each request is approved by the administrator.

  3. Issue the receipt

    One receipt goes to the whole building or to chosen apartments. Residents are notified straight away.

  4. Mark it paid

    Neighbours pay the administrator outside Baytak. The administrator stamps each apartment Paid, and everyone can see it.

Questions people ask.

Something else? Support is one click away.

Does Baytak take payments?

No. Baytak records receipts and who has paid. Residents pay their administrator outside the app, and the administrator marks the apartment paid.

Can a receipt be changed after it is issued?

No. Receipts cannot be edited or deleted once issued. If one is wrong, ask your administrator how they want to correct it.

Which currencies does Baytak use?

USD and LBP. Each amount shows its own currency, and the two are never added together.

How do I sign in?

Enter your phone number and the 6-digit code that Baytak sends you by SMS. There is no password.

Who can see my phone number?

Your building administrator sees your name, phone number, apartment, emergency contact and payment status. The privacy policy lists everything we collect and who receives it.

Is Baytak available in Arabic?

Not yet. The app is in English today. Arabic with right-to-left layout is planned.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. Read what gets deleted.

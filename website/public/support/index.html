Common questions

My sign-in code did not arrive

Check that you entered your number with its country code, for example +961. Codes can take a minute to arrive, and you can ask for a new one after a short wait. If it still does not arrive, write to us with your number's country code and what you saw.

I cannot find my building

Buildings are listed nearest first, using your phone's location, so allow location access for Baytak. If your building is still missing, its administrator has to register it first.

My request to join is still pending

Your building administrator approves each request. Ask them to open Baytak and approve it.

I asked for administrator access

Baytak reviews every administrator request. It stays pending until we approve it. Write to us if you have been waiting a while.

A receipt amount looks wrong

Receipts cannot be changed once issued, so ask your administrator how they want to correct it. Baytak only shows what the administrator issued.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account in the app and choose Delete account. Read what gets deleted.

How do I disconnect ChatGPT?

Disconnect Baytak in ChatGPT's settings, or sign out of the Baytak app to end every session. More about Baytak in ChatGPT.

I am an administrator and want to leave

An administrator whose building still has residents or receipts must hand it over first. Write to us and we will help with that.

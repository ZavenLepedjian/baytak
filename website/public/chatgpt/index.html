Baytak in ChatGPT

Ask what you owe, see your receipts, or run your building's fees, in plain words.

What you can do

Baytak in ChatGPT shows only your own apartment, or your own building if you are its administrator. Residents get three actions, and approved building administrators get all of them.

Residents

  • What you owe now, per currency, with the unpaid receipts
  • Your receipts, paid and unpaid
  • Your apartment, building, request status and role

Building administrators

Look things up

  • Who has paid a receipt, apartment by apartment
  • Residents waiting to join, and approved apartments
  • The currency and fields of your receipts

Make changes

  • Mark apartments paid or unpaid
  • Approve or reject a join request
  • Send a reminder to apartments that have not paid, once a day per receipt
  • Issue receipts in USD, LBP or both, after you review a preview

Signing in

When you connect Baytak, ChatGPT opens Baytak's own sign-in page. Enter the phone number you use in the Baytak app and the 6-digit code sent to it. Then you see what ChatGPT will be allowed to do, and you choose Allow or Deny. Your code is typed only on Baytak's page, never in the chat.

Changes need your confirmation

Issuing a receipt shows a preview first: the amounts, the apartments and a note that it cannot be undone. Nothing is issued until you confirm. Receipts, once issued, cannot be edited or deleted, so review every preview.

What ChatGPT receives

ChatGPT receives the results of the Baytak actions used in your conversation: your apartment, receipts and payment status, and for administrators, residents' names, apartment numbers and payment status in their building. Baytak never sends phone numbers or emergency contacts, and does not receive your ChatGPT conversations. ChatGPT's answers can be wrong, so treat the Baytak app as the record. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Disconnecting

Disconnect Baytak in ChatGPT's settings at any time, or sign out of the Baytak app to end every session. Access lasts 1 hour at a time, and a connection ends after at most 30 days.

Questions to try

  • “Do I owe anything for my building this month?”
  • “Show my last receipts and which ones are paid.”
  • For administrators: “Who hasn’t paid this month’s building receipt yet?”
